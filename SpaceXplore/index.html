🛰️ NASA MISSION ARCHITECTURE

Explore the four segments of a science mission, based on NASA's Design and Develop Science Missions educational resource.

🚀 Launch Segment

The launch vehicle and related services that carry the spacecraft into space.

🛰️ Space Segment

Spacecraft, orbiters, landers, rovers and instruments used to collect data.

📡 Ground Segment

Ground stations and mission control communicate with spacecraft and receive data.

🔬 User Segment

Scientists and the public use collected mission data for research and education.
